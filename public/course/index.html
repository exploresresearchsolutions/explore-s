FAQ

Questions, answered clearly

Still unsure about something? Reach out and our counsellors will help.

Do your programs really offer a Job Guarantee?

Yes. Our eligible programs are backed by a structured Job Guarantee placement framework, with dedicated support to help you get hired after successful completion. Speak to our counsellor for the exact terms of your chosen program.

Who is eligible for these programs?

Eligibility varies by program — including educational background, pass-out year and age limit. Our counsellor will confirm exactly which program you qualify for during your enquiry call.

Do I need a strong technical background to join?

Not necessarily. Some of our programs, like Windows Admin, are designed for IT and non-IT beginners alike, while advanced tracks like AI/ML are best suited for IT graduates. We'll help you find the right fit.

Is there an age limit to enrol?

Yes, each program has its own age eligibility criteria. Our counsellor will guide you on which program matches your profile.

How much does a program cost?

Program fees depend on the course and any current offers. Our counsellor will walk you through complete fee details on a personal call — simply click "Enquire Now" to get started.

Is placement support available across India?

Yes, we offer Pan-India placement support so you're not limited to opportunities in a single city or region.

How do I get started?

Just fill out the enquiry form or message us directly on WhatsApp. Our counsellor will call you to understand your background and recommend the best-fit program.
